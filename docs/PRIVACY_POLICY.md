## Short version
- You can play without giving us your name, email, birthday or any other personal detail.
- There is no chat, no friends list with free text, and no way to send messages to other players.
- We store your farm so it is not lost. We do not sell data. Ads are not personalised.

## What we store and why
| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Random account ID (created automatically, anonymous) | Save your farm in the cloud | Supabase (EU region) |
| Farm progress: coins, gems, items, buildings, missions, display name chosen from a word list | Run the game, prevent cheating | Supabase |
| History of in-game changes (coins earned/spent) | Fairness, fixing problems, refunds | Supabase |
| Purchase receipts (order ID, product, time) | Give you what was bought, exactly once | Supabase + Google Play |
| Parent email – **only if a parent adds it** in the Parents area | Restore the farm on a new phone | Supabase Auth |
| On-device save file and settings | Play offline | Your phone only |

We do **not** collect: real names, birthdays, contacts, photos, location, microphone, or free text.

## Ads
Optional video ads, only when the player chooses to watch one for a reward. Ads are requested as
child-directed, non-personalised, content rating G. The ad provider (Google AdMob) may process device
information to deliver and count ads, under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Purchases
Purchases go through Google Play. We never see card details. Every purchase is behind a parental check.

## Deleting your data
Settings → Parents → "Delete my data" deletes the account and all cloud data immediately.
Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone. You can also write to [contact email].

## Children
The game is designed for children and families. We follow the principle of collecting as little as
possible. Parents can contact us at any time to see or delete their child's data.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and show a notice in the game.
